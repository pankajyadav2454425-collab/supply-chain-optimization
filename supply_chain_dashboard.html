Key insights
Warehouse holding cost ($15.63M) represents 99.6% of total cost. Freight ($69.6K) is only 0.4%. The dominant optimization lever is not carrier selection — it is reducing inventory volume at PLANT03, which alone holds $14.8M in goods.
PLANT03 handles 92.7% of all orders (8,541 of 9,215) but has a daily capacity of only 1,013 — an effective utilization of 843%. This is a single point of failure and the network's biggest operational risk.
Carrier optimization reassigns 1,436 orders from V444_1 to V444_0 and others, capturing a freight saving of $4,304 (6.2%). While small in absolute terms, this represents the highest-quality optimization possible purely on carrier selection.
PLANT08 has a late delivery rate of 17.6% (18 of 102 orders), the worst in the network. With a daily capacity of just 14 orders, it is permanently overloaded at 728% utilization — the root cause of its delays.
